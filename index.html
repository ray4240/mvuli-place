The development

A place to start, ten minutes from everywhere you already go.

Mvuli Place offers contemporary 10-story living situated along Nyakinyua Road, Riruta—just 500 meters from Naivasha Road. Perfectly crafted studios and 1-bedroom apartments give you seamless connectivity to the heart of Nairobi.

Proudly developed under NACHU and constructed by Placemakers Kenya Ltd, Mvuli Place features 24/7 power backup, secure borehole water, CCTV surveillance, professional security, high-speed lifts, and ample resident parking.

Good questions to ask

Clarity before commitment.

Use these answers as a starting point, and ask the project team to confirm the details that matter to you.

Speak with the team ↗
Does sending an enquiry reserve an apartment?

No. An enquiry only starts a conversation. Ask the project team about available units, the sale agreement and the current booking process.

Are the prices and payment stages final?

The prices shown are starting figures from the project information. The displayed 20% / 20% / 50% / 10% schedule is indicative. Request the latest written price list and payment terms before paying.

Are the pictures photographs of finished apartments?

No. The gallery contains architectural and interior renderings. Confirm the current specifications and finishes with the project team.

Can I see a specific floor or unit?

Ask for the latest availability and the plans for the floor you are interested in. The unit numbering in this site is an illustrative planning aid until a confirmed register is supplied.

Is the rental return guaranteed?

No. The investment section shows a gross illustration based on estimated rent and indicative starting price. Actual income and costs will vary.
