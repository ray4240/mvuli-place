The development

Ten floors, one address, a lot of Nairobi within reach.

Mvuli Place is a 10-floor apartment block on Nyakinyua Road, 500 metres off Naivasha Road in Riruta — 11km west of the CBD, and a short matatu ride from Kilimani, Westlands, Upper Hill and town.

It's a joint effort: NACHU brings its cooperative housing network and delivery track record, Placemakers Kenya Ltd is building it. The result is a mid-market apartment block aimed at first-time buyers, young professionals, and investors who want a foothold in Nairobi property without a CBD price tag.

The building itself covers the basics well — backup power, a borehole, CCTV and manned security, fire systems, high-speed lifts — rather than chasing amenities that push the price out of reach.
